### Configuration

- `CATALYST_IMPLEMENTATION_NAME`
- `CATALYST_IMPLEMENTATION_PATHS`

### Debugging

- `CATALYST_DEBUG=1`
- `PARAVIEW_LOG_CATALYST_VERBOSITY=INFO`

### ParaView Visualization Pipeline

```yaml
catalyst:
    scripts:
        filename: myparaview-catalyst-script.py
```
